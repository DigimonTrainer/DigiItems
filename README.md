Ever wanted to just craft Digimon Items, even the Digimon? Strangely specific, I know.
Well, now you can.

This mod is designed to take a whole new twist on the Digimon Modscape. Instead of teaming up with a wild Digimon, you can craft the Digimon!

There are:
- Digimon
- Appmon
- App Chips
- Crests
- DigiEggs(Digimentals for subbed users :) )
- DiM Cards
- Digivices, every single kind appearing in the anime, plus the D-Storage for Liberator Fans
- DigiCards(for use with the D-Ark, you even have the Blue Card!)
- ChromeDigizoid
- Spirits

Each one has their own crafting recipe and obtainment method. Try to find them all! (Well, will have their own crafting recipe, I'm still in heavy beta phase. Majority of items are here, but I've got to write the recipes. One step at a time.)

Requires:
- CraftedCore
- woodwalkers

You can find these on Modrinth.

Functions:
- Right click Digimon item to become an Allay(will be a Digimon when I get to modeling)
- Right click a DiM Card with a Vital Bracelet in your inventory to get a random Digimon that is loaded on the DiM.
- Right click Appmon item to become an allay(will be an Appmon when I get to modeling)
